## Notes

WT2003HP8 is made only as WT2003HP8-32N (QFN32 4 × 4 mm, section 4.3); the SOP-16 pinout in section 4.1 belongs to WT2003H0 / H4-16S. Pin 2 is RXD/KEY9/DATA1/CLK2 as the pin drawing gives it (the table prints CL2K). Pitch 0.4 mm, body 4.0 mm, exposed pad 2.65 mm from the QFN32 drawing (section 10.3, page 27); the pad is internal ground and must be grounded.
