## Notes

The supplied datasheet is TPS65381-Q1 (SLVSBC4G); its orderable part is TPS65381QDAPRQ1 (marked NRND), so that is the part added. TPS65381A-Q1 has its own datasheet and is not added from this one. Body 11.00 × 6.20 mm (Device Information). The thermal pad joins GND and PGND (pin table): `EP` is GND. Pin types: PWR pins that the device supplies (VCP, VDD5, VDD3/5, VSOUT1) are power outputs; CP1 / CP2 / SDN6 are passive; NRES / ENDRV are open drain.
