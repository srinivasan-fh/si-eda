## Notes

The supplied datasheet is the DRV835x family (SLVSDY6A). It has no DRV8353RTAWRQ1: its 40-pin RTA parts are DRV8353SRTAR (SPI) and DRV8353HRTAR (hardware pins); the SPI part is added. The pin table gives the thermal pad no net, so `EP` is left unnamed (EP).
