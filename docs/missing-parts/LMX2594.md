Use the datasheet's pin name exactly (no spaces; `/` is fine). Ground and supply pins are `PowerIn`; regulator outputs are `PowerOut`; open-drain outputs (nFAULT, nINT …) are `OpenCollector`; NC pins are `NoConnect`.
