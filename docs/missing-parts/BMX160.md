## Notes

Lands from Figure 38 (landing pattern, page 106): pins 1–4 and 8–11 are 0.675 × 0.25 mm at 0.5 mm pitch, centres ±1.2625 mm (inner edges 0.925 mm from the centre); pins 5–7 and 12–14 are 0.25 × 0.675 mm at 0.5 mm pitch, centres ±1.0125 mm (inner edges 0.675 mm from the centre). Body 3.0 × 2.5 mm (section 5.1). Pins 10 and 11 are do-not-connect.
